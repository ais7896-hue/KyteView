Everyone has folders filled with similarly named files like `Quote_v1.xlsx`, `Quote_FINAL.xlsx`, or `Pitch_Deck_Rev3.pptx`. Double-clicking each file and waiting for bulky Excel or PowerPoint processes to spin up wastes minutes of focus every single day.

## Why the Built-in Windows Explorer Preview Pane Falls Short

The native File Explorer preview pane permanently eats up horizontal screen real estate, fails to render Office files if Microsoft Office isn't installed, and lacks syntax highlighting for source code, Markdown rendering, or archive inspection.

## Instant Spacebar Inspection with KyteView

1. **Dedicated 16:9 PPTX Canvas & Slide Outlines**: Select any presentation in Explorer and tap `Space` to view embedded high-res slide thumbnails and text outlines in 2–5ms.
2. **Rust-Powered XLSX Spreadsheet Engine**: Powered by the ultra-fast `calamine` parser, even multi-sheet financial workbooks render into clean scrollable tables almost instantaneously.
3. **Drag-and-Drop Single File Extraction from ZIP / 7z / RAR**: Tap `Space` on an archive, locate the exact contract or image inside the tree, and drag it straight onto your desktop.
